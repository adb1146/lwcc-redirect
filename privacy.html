Privacy Policy — LWCC Injured Worker Companion

Last updated: June 2026. This app is a proof-of-concept by PS Advisory for LWCC.

The LWCC Injured Worker Companion ("the app") lets an injured worker connect their own medical record through their health system's secure sign-in (SMART on FHIR / Epic MyChart) and view their diagnoses, medications, lab results, allergies, and immunizations, with optional plain-language explanations and a document scanner.

Current build (beta / TestFlight)

This beta connects to Epic's public FHIR sandbox. Testers sign in as a demo patient and see test data — not real patient records.

What the app accesses

  • Health record data from your health system, only after you sign in and explicitly approve which categories the app may see. This is shown to you in the app; it is not sold or shared.
  • Camera / photo library (only when you choose to scan a document), to read a photographed work note, bill, or form.

Authentication & storage

Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. Access tokens are stored only in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain) and are never sent to us. We do not have a server that stores your health record.

AI features

The "Explain" and "Scan a document" features send the minimum necessary clinical values (or the document image you chose) to a secure server-side proxy that calls an AI model to generate a plain-language explanation. Only de-identified clinical values are sent for "Explain" (no name, date of birth, or medical record number). No personal health information is logged.

Data we do not collect

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal data.
